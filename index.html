# Our Story

Built on experience. Driven by possibility.
Engineered for what comes next.

Since 2007, X Spark has been driven by a simple belief: technology should do more than keep businesses running. It should help them move forward.

What began as a commitment to solving real-world technology challenges has evolved into a software engineering studio focused on building intelligent digital products, modernising infrastructure, and helping businesses unlock new possibilities through technology.

Over the years, we have worked across industries, developed solutions to complex problems, and seen first-hand how the right technology can transform the way organisations operate, compete, and grow. That experience has shaped how we approach every challenge: understand the problem, engineer the right solution, and build for the future.

Today, we are embracing the next era of technology. Combining software engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud engineering, DevOps, and cybersecurity, we bring together the capabilities needed to turn ambitious ideas into practical, scalable, and secure solutions.

But our vision extends beyond the technology itself. We believe innovation should create opportunity, strengthen businesses, and contribute to a more inclusive digital economy across Africa and beyond.

We are building X Spark around that belief: a new generation of engineering where intelligence meets execution, security is built in from the beginning, and every solution is designed with purpose.

We don't build technology for the sake of technology. We engineer what comes next.

This is X Spark.

01. COBRO IMS — INTELLIGENT INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

Bringing Intelligence to Inventory Operations.

Enterprise Operations & Software

Transforming complex inventory operations into a connected digital platform. Developed for Cobro Concrete, the system brings multi-warehouse stock control, procurement, receiving, transfers, stocktakes, dispatch and reporting into one integrated operational environment. Barcode and QR workflows help connect physical inventory to accurate digital records.

Capabilities

  • Inventory management
  • Procurement
  • Warehouse operations
  • QR/barcode workflows
  • Systems integration

02. X SPARK OS O — THE INTELLIGENT OFFICE OPERATING SYSTEM

Re-engineering the Business Operating System.

Proprietary Technology & Cybersecurity

Engineering a controlled, security-focused desktop operating environment designed for modern business. Combining Linux platform engineering, employee and administrator separation, role-based access controls, signed software updates, system hardening and automated provisioning to create a more controlled and dependable workplace computing experience.

Capabilities

  • Operating systems
  • Linux engineering
  • Cybersecurity
  • Role-based access control
  • Signed updates
  • Automation

03. SALESDAM — INTELLIGENT CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP MANAGEMENT

Turning Customer Relationships into Business Intelligence.

Enterprise CRM & Business Management

Engineering a comprehensive CRM platform that centralises customer relationships, sales pipelines, business processes and team activities in one integrated environment. Designed to help organisations manage leads, track opportunities, coordinate customer interactions, oversee users and monitor business performance through intelligent workflows and centralised reporting.

Capabilities

  • CRM
  • Sales pipeline management
  • Customer lifecycle management
  • Workflow automation
  • User administration
  • Business intelligence
