# X Spark - Public Information (for website assistant)

## What X Spark does
X Spark helps businesses build and improve digital solutions. This can include:
- Websites, web apps, mobile apps, and custom software
- AI and automation solutions
- Cloud, DevOps, and DevSecOps support
- XS Card (a digital business card and networking solution)

## How a project usually starts
1. **First conversation:** you share what you need, your goals, and any important context.
2. **Understanding the work:** X Spark discusses scope, users, requirements, and priorities in plain terms.
3. **Proposal and plan:** X Spark outlines the recommended approach, scope, timeline expectations, and next steps for agreement.
4. **Build and review:** the solution is designed, built, and reviewed in stages with feedback.
5. **Testing and launch:** the work is tested and prepared for release.
6. **Handover and support:** after launch, X Spark can help with handover and ongoing support if needed.

## What helps X Spark get started
- Your goals and the problem you want to solve
- Who will use the solution and who makes decisions
- Any ideas about features, scope, content, or examples
- Your timeline or target dates (if you have them)
- Access to existing systems, content, or information needed for the work (when relevant)

## Timelines and pricing
- Timelines and pricing depend on the scope and requirements of the work.
- There is no fixed price or fixed timeline for every project.
- X Spark confirms timelines and pricing after discussing your needs.
